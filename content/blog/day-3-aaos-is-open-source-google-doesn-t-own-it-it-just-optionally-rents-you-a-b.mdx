This trips people up constantly: "Android Automotive" and "Google's car OS" get used interchangeably. They're not the same thing.

AAOS itself — CarService, VHAL, the whole framework — is AOSP. Open source, free, no licensing fee. Any automaker can take it and ship a fully working infotainment system with their own app store, their own maps, their own voice assistant, and zero Google inside. This is exactly why it's common in China, where Google's services aren't viable, and in cost-sensitive EV programs.

Google Automotive Services (GAS) — marketed today as "Google built-in" — is the separate, optional layer that sits on top. It's what actually adds Play Store, Play Services, Google Maps, and now a Gemini-powered Assistant. To get it, an OEM has to license and certify with Google. It's shipping today in Volvo, Polestar, GM, Honda, and Renault.
